The Hopf fibration describes a 3-sphere mapping into a 2-sphere with circles as fibers, representing a fundamental fiber bundle in topology.

By stereographically projecting intertwined circular fibers into three-dimensional space, intricate toroidal link systems emerge dynamically with vibrant color transformations.
