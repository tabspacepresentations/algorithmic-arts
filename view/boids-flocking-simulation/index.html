Boids is an artificial life simulation that mimics the flocking behavior of birds, based on three simple steering rules: separation, alignment, and cohesion.

This enhanced algorithmic art piece features smooth animated flocking dynamics, dynamic color shimmering, and fully interactive touch pinch-to-zoom, swipe, and wheel controls directly within your browser viewport.
