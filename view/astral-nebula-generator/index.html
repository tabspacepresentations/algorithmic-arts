The Astral Nebula is a real-time procedural simulation of deep-space cosmic clouds and interstellar nurseries. By algorithmically rendering multi-layered luminous gas structures and drifting stellar dust, it captures the majestic glow of ionized matter reacting to stellar winds.

Interwoven with a twinkling background starfield and dynamic color-shifting properties, this simulation recreates the infinite depth of the cosmos directly within your browser viewport.
