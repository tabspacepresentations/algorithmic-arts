The Aurora Borealis (commonly known as the Northern Lights) is a breathtaking natural light display occurring high in Earth's atmosphere. It is caused when energetic charged particles from the magnetosphere collide with gas atoms and molecules in the upper atmosphere, releasing vibrant curtains of green, pink, and violet light.

This digital simulation captures the mesmerizing geomagnetic radiance, undulating light ribbons, and twinkling starfields of the polar night sky directly within your browser viewport.
