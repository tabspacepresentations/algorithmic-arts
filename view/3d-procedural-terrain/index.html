The 3D Procedural Terrain is a real-time mathematical wireframe simulation that generates infinite topographical landscapes. By mapping multi-frequency sine and cosine wave functions onto a dynamic grid, it constructs rolling valleys and mountainous elevations that continuously fly forward.

Featuring interactive rotation controls and fluid perspective scaling, this simulation delivers an immersive, high-performance visualization of generative digital cartography directly within your browser viewport.
