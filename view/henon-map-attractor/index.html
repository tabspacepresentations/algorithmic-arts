The Hénon map is a discrete-time dynamical system that exhibits chaotic behavior, mapping a point (x, y) to a new coordinate based on quadratic non-linear equations.

As one of the most studied examples of strange attractors, repeated iterations reveal an intricate, self-similar fractal structure that captures the core principles of chaos theory.
