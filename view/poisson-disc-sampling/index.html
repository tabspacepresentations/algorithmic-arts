Poisson disc sampling is an algorithmic technique used to generate points that are tightly packed together while maintaining a specified minimum distance separation constraint.

By producing blue noise spatial distribution patterns, it generates naturally spaced point configurations widely used in procedural graphics, computer vision, and organic texture generation.
