An Apollonian gasket is a fractal generated by starting with three mutually tangent circles and iteratively filling all remaining interstitial spaces with mutually tangent circles based on Descartes' theorem.

This enhanced algorithmic art piece features smooth animated breathing pulses, dynamic color shimmering, and fully interactive pan, swipe, and zoom controls directly within your browser viewport.
