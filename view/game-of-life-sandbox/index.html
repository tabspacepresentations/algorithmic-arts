John Conway's Game of Life is a cellular automaton governed by simple rules where cells are born, live, or die based on the state of their eight neighbors, creating complex, emergent patterns.

This enhanced algorithmic arts piece features an interactive simulation canvas, allowing you to paint initial patterns, featuring dynamic color shimmering, and fully interactive touch pinch-to-zoom, swipe, and wheel controls directly within your browser viewport.
