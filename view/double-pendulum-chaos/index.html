A double pendulum is a physical pendulum with another pendulum attached to its end, exhibiting rich unpredictable chaotic motion highly sensitive to initial conditions.

This enhanced algorithmic arts piece simulates nonlinear Lagrangian equations of motion, featuring smooth animated trace trails, dynamic color shimmering, and fully interactive touch pinch-to-zoom, swipe, and wheel controls directly within your browser viewport.
