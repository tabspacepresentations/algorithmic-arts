An epicycloid is a plane curve produced by tracing the path of a chosen point on the circumference of a circle—called an epicycle—that rolls without slipping around the outside of a fixed fixed circle.

This enhanced algorithmic arts piece simulates rolling harmonic curves, featuring smooth animated orbital traces, dynamic color shimmering, and fully interactive touch pinch-to-zoom, swipe, and wheel controls directly within your browser viewport.
