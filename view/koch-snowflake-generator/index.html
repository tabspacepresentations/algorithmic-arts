The Koch snowflake is one of the earliest known fractals, constructed by iteratively replacing the middle section of line segments with triangular peaks.

Featuring infinite perimeter enclosed within a finite area, this recursive geometry creates mesmerizing crystalline structures enriched with shifting color spectrums.
