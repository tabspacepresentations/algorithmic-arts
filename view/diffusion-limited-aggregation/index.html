Diffusion-limited aggregation (DLA) is the process whereby particles undergoing random walk due to Brownian motion cluster together to form aggregates of such chaotic, fractal structures.

This enhanced algorithmic arts piece simulates stochastic random walkers latching onto growing fractal clusters, featuring smooth animated nucleation expansion, dynamic color shimmering, and fully interactive touch pinch-to-zoom, swipe, and wheel controls directly within your browser viewport.
